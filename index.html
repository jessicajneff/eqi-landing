The Assessment

The EQ-i 2.0: The World's Leading Emotional Intelligence Assessment

The EQ-i 2.0 is the most widely used measure of emotional intelligence in the world. It provides a detailed picture of your EQ across 15 competencies organized into five composite areas — helping you understand not just your overall emotional intelligence, but where you're strongest and where targeted development would have the greatest impact.

Even highly accomplished clinicians are often surprised by what their EQ-i 2.0 results reveal — particularly around areas like stress tolerance, impulse control, and interpersonal relationships under pressure. The personalized report and coaching session help you understand not just what your profile shows, but how to apply it in your day-to-day leadership.

Frequently Asked Questions

What you need to know.

What is the EQ-i 2.0?

The EQ-i 2.0 (Emotional Quotient Inventory) is the world's most widely used measure of emotional intelligence. It assesses 15 EQ competencies across five composite areas and provides a detailed report of your unique emotional intelligence profile — including strengths and areas for development.

How is this different from the Navigating Conflict workshop?

The Navigating Conflict workshop uses the TKI to explore your conflict-handling style. This workshop uses the EQ-i 2.0 to examine the broader emotional intelligence competencies that underlie all your leadership interactions — including but not limited to conflict. Together they form a comprehensive picture of your interpersonal effectiveness.

What will my coaching session look like?

Your 1-hour private session is tailored specifically to your EQ-i 2.0 results. Your coach will help you understand your profile, explore how it shows up in your clinical leadership context, and develop a concrete personalized action plan. Sessions are conducted via Zoom.

How much time does this require in total?

The total time commitment is approximately 3 hours: 20–30 minutes for the EQ-i 2.0 assessment (completed beforehand at your own pace), 2 hours for the live group webinar, and 1 hour for your private coaching session, which is flexibly scheduled after the webinar.

Is this only for physicians?

Not at all. The workshop is designed for physicians, nurses, and healthcare professionals in leadership roles across all specialties. The learning is richer with representation from a variety of roles and perspectives.

What is the cancellation policy?

All cancellations must be submitted in writing — phone cancellations are not accepted. Requests for cancellation must be received at least 2 weeks prior to the scheduled course date to be eligible for a refund.

What if I can't attend the live webinar?

No problem — the webinar will be recorded and available for 90 days. However, we recommend attending live to participate in the Q&A and group discussion, as the live format adds significant value to the learning experience.

Will this course be offered again?

Yes — we plan to offer this course periodically. Each cohort is intentionally limited in size to protect the quality of private coaching sessions.
